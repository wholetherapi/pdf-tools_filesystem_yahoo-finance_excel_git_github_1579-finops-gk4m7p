# Larkspur Field Research - Finance Operations

This repository is the finance operations desk for Larkspur Field Research.

- `finance/travel-expense-register.md` - the travel reimbursement register
  (pending and approved claims, conversion policy, archive rules).
- `receipts/` - travel receipts filed by the travel desk.

The register is consumed by the reimbursement importer; keep its section
structure intact.
